The game is played over 7 rounds. Each round takes place in 3 phases:

Discuss your strategy at the beginning of every round...

If there is a Reroll token in the Current Altitude space (as there

--- 2 DICE PLACEMENT — GENERAL RULES ---

A  Take turns. The arrow in the Current Altitude screen will indicate
   who plays first. For example, the Pilot (blue) begins the first round.

   on a FREE space on the Control Panel.

   in blue spaces, and the Co-Pilot in the orange spaces. Some spaces
   are both blue and orange; either player can place dice there.

C  Respect the number constraints. For example, the Co-Pilot may only
   place a 1 or 2 in the first Flaps space.

D  The majority of spaces have colour AND/OR number constraints.
   For example, only the Pilot can place a die in the Brakes section,
   AND it must be a die showing the number 2.

E  The Radio spaces have colour - but not number - constraints.

F  The Concentration spaces don't have any constraints (any player
   may play a die of any value here).

### 补充说明

**这页讲什么：** **一整局的时间结构** + **前两阶段** 怎么进行。

**7 轮 × 3 阶段：**

| 阶段 | 要点 |
|------|------|
| 1 策略+掷骰 | 可聊策略，**禁聊点数**；各掷 4 骰；之后静默至本轮结束 |
| 2 放置 | 轮流每次 1 骰；遵守颜色/点数/双色槽规则 |
| 3 轮末 | 见 p.9 |

**重掷 token：** 高度窗格上有则收入供应区；轮内任一人可消耗，**双方** 各可重掷任意颗自己骰子 **一次**。

- **双色槽（p.4 图示 F）：** 底部 **3 个** 集中精力放骰位（蓝橙双色 ☕）+ 姿态/引擎（见 p.4 正文）
- **仅颜色：** 无线电  
- **无约束：** 集中精力放骰位  
- **颜色+点数：** 刹车、襟翼、起落架等

ACTIONS

The dice produce different effects depending on their value and
the spaces on which they are placed.

MANDATORY ACTIONS

Action spaces with a [!] are mandatory; all other Action spaces
are not. Every round, each player must place one die on the Axis,

You immediately lose the game if, at the end of a round, there

In real life, airplanes work on 3 axes: yaw, pitch, and roll.
The illustration you see here is a simplification, as it only
represents the roll axis.

VICTORY CONDITION C
At the end of the last round, your plane must be completely

### 补充说明

**这页讲什么：** 「行动」总述 + **强制槽** + **姿态 Axis** 完整规则。

**强制（每轮）：** 机长、副驾各 1 骰在 **姿态**、各 1 骰在 **引擎**。轮末缺任一颜色 → **立即判负**。不必优先放置。

**姿态结算（第二颗姿态骰放上时）：**

| 情况 | 效果 |
|------|------|
| 两点相同 | 不动 |
| 不同 | 转 **差值** 格，偏向 **大点** 一方；**轮末不复位** |
| 箭头到/过 X | **尾旋，立即判负** |

**胜利条件 C（预告）：** 最后一轮结束时姿态 **完全水平（0）**。

**策略提示：** 姿态是「即时双骰结算」机制的第一个；顺序影响后续引擎推进时的姿态检查（见 p.15 Turns）。

### 原始文本（OCR）

```
ENGINES [!]

Depending on the power you assigned to the engines, the Airplane
will advance... or not!

As soon as the second die is placed, add together the 2 dice played
onto the Engine spaces; this is your speed. Then:

If the sum is less than the weakest (blue) of the 2 Aerodynamics
markers on the Speed Gauge, leave the Approach Track in place

If the sum is between the 2 Aerodynamics markers, advance the
Approach Track one space.

If the sum is greater than the highest (orange) of the 2

Examples: 1+3=4 → STOP | 3+4=7 → advance 1 | 6+4=10 → advance 2

COLLISION
If one or more Airplane tokens move into the Current Position
space, you're still alive!

If there are Airplane tokens in the Current Position space and
you have to advance the Approach Track, you have had a collision,
and you've lost the game!

OVERSHOOT
If the airport is in the Current Position space and you have to
advance the Approach Track, you have overshot the airport, and
you've lost the game!

6
```

### 补充说明

**这页讲什么：** **引擎 = 速度 = 进近轨移动**；以及两种 instant-lose。

**速度三档（第二颗引擎骰放上时，对照蓝/橙气动标记）：**

| 引擎和 | 进近轨 |
|--------|--------|
| **<** 蓝标记 | 不动（0 格） |
| **≥ 蓝 且 ≤ 橙** | 前进 1 格 |
| **>** 橙标记 | 前进 2 格 |

**示例（p.6 图）：** 蓝在 5、橙在 8 → 4 停、7 走 1、10 走 2。

**碰撞 vs 安全：** 轨滑动后 token **刚进入** 当前位置 OK；**已在** 当前位置且还必须前进 → 碰撞败。

**冲出：** 机场已在当前位置，仍须前进 → 冲出败。

**物理模型：** Approach Track **整卡下滑**，飞机 token 随格移动；Current Position 是面板上的 **固定窗口**。

### 补充说明

**这页讲什么：** 三种 **放置即生效** 的行动——无线电、起落架、以及气动标记的意义。

**无线电：**

- 机长 **1** 槽，副驾 **2** 槽，任意点数

### 补充说明

**这页讲什么：** 进近 **两种异常** + **着陆轮** 引擎规则切换。

**过早到机场（等待航线）：** 位置已是机场，高度还未到飞机图标 → 须再玩完整轮次，**进近轨不得再前进**，速度保持 **0 前进**（< 蓝标记）。

**未及时到达：** 高度已到飞机图标，位置还不是机场 → **坠毁，判负**。

**着陆轮：** 双窗口对齐时开始；此轮 **第二颗引擎骰** 结算时：

- **不再** 比气动标记  
- 改为：引擎和 **<** 红刹车标记（等价刹车 **>** 速度）  
- 至少须部署过刹车 2（标记不能停在 2 以左）  

**与 p.6 关系：** 正常轮次仍用蓝/橙标记决定 0/1/2 格；仅着陆轮改比刹车。
